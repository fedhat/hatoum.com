Best Mini Spot Welder? Review of the Malectrics Mini Spot Welder

December 17, 2021 · 1 min read

TL;DR

I reviewed the Malectrics Mini Spot Welder (v3) on my YouTube channel Fed Learns to Make. Compared to other mini spot welders, I found the Malectrics to be much better built, designed and safer. It's not the cheapest (nor the most expensive), but is, in my opinion, the best you can get under $200.

Website: Malectrics.eu
